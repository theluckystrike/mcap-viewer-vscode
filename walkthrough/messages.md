# Messages, seek and plot

- Click a topic in the list on the left. Its messages show 50 at a time, with the log time and size of each.
- Click a message to read every field. Long arrays show their first 64 items.
- Drag the slider to jump to a moment in the recording. The < and > buttons show the page before or after.
- The plot shows one number field of the topic over the whole recording. Pick another field in its list, and click the plot to seek there.
